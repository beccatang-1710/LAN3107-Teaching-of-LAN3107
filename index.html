Lesson 2

Making Small Talk — Hobbies & Leisure Activities

Learning Objectives 學習目標

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • use active listening skills and respond appropriately in casual conversation
  • engage in small talk
  • use appropriate intonation in questions
  • start and maintain a casual conversation about hobbies and leisure activities using useful expressions

The Universal Starter:

"How was your weekend? Did you get up to anything fun?"

Progress / Practice:

"How's your [hobby/practice] going? (e.g. guitar practice, marathon training)"

General Leisure Inquiries:

"What do you usually like to do to unwind after work / on weekends?"

Recent Pastimes:

"Have you had a chance to [activity: go hiking / see that new movie] lately?"

Work in pairs in 5 minutes.

Student A: Choose a starter above to ask your partner.

Student B: Reply using an appropriate response phrase below.

Take turns starting the conversation. You have 5 minutes to practise.

Response Phrases (To Answer):

  • "It was pretty chill—mostly just caught up on some [reading / shows]."
  • "I've actually really been getting into [baking / bouldering] lately."
  • "It's going well! I try to practice a couple of times a week."
  • "Not as much as I'd like, honestly—work has been pretty busy."
  • "Yeah, I actually went to [place / event] last weekend—it was great!"

Short Reaction Tokens

"Really?" | "Oh yeah?" | "Wow!" | "That's great!" | "That sounds fun / amazing!" | "That's impressive!"

Echo Word Formula

Pattern:

"[Keyword]? That sounds [adjective]!"

"[Keyword]? That's a great way to [benefit]!"

Examples

Outdoor / Travel:

A: "I went hiking in Sai Kung."
B: "Sai Kung? That sounds refreshing!"

Creative Arts:

A: "I took up photography recently."
B: "Photography? That sounds fascinating!"

Health / Wellness:

A: "I've started doing yoga every morning."
B: "Yoga every morning? That's a great way to start the day!"

Work in pairs. You have 10 minutes.

Student A: Share a hobby, recent activity, or weekend plan using the Topic Prompts (or your own ideas).

Student B: React using an Echo Word Formula:

  • "[Keyword]? That sounds [adjective]!"
  • "[Keyword]? That's a great way to [benefit]!"

Switch roles and try different combinations.

Lesson 2 Activity

Describing a Hobby or Leisure Activity

In acknowledgement of Nancy Ng

Instructions:

  • Choose one model sample below as the description of your favourite hobby/ leisure activity/ pastime. Share it with your partner. Learn the underlined adjectives for describing the hobby/ pastime/ leisure activity.

Model Samples

1. Hiking

Hiking in the countryside is my favourite weekend pastime because it clears my mind. Last Saturday, I tackled the scenic Dragon's Back trail. As I climbed up the ridge, the cool mountain breeze and breathtaking coastal views made the physical effort worthwhile. Reaching the peak left me feeling completely refreshed.

(50 words)

2. Street Photography

Street photography has been my creative outlet ever since I bought my first digital camera. On weekends, I wander through vibrant city streets, searching for candid expressions and striking light. Pressing the shutter at the perfect instant to capture raw everyday life gives me an unforgettable rush of artistic satisfaction.

(50 words)

3. Acoustic Guitar

Last weekend, I started learning how to play the guitar. I've always wanted to learn a musical instrument, so I signed up for a beginner's class. So far, I've learned a few basic chords and practised playing some simple songs. Although it's challenging, I'm really enjoying the experience.

(48 words)

4. Morning Yoga

I practice yoga weekly to maintain mental clarity and physical balance. Rolling out my mat at sunrise, I move through a sequence of gentle stretches and deep breathing. Transitioning smoothly between poses eases my morning stiffness and leaves me feeling grounded, calm, and energized for the day.

(47 words)

5. Board Gaming

Hosting board game nights with my friends is my favourite leisure activity. When we gather around the dining table, we immerse ourselves in complex strategy games, carefully trading resources and planning tactics. The room fills with laughter and playful banter, making our intense tabletop battles both intellectually stimulating and fun.

(50 words)

6. Artisanal Baking

Baking sourdough bread is a rewarding hobby that I pursue every weekend. I genuinely enjoy the tactile experience of measuring ingredients and rhythmically kneading the dough on the kitchen counter. Watching the loaf rise in a hot oven and sharing the warm, crusty bread with my family brings immense happiness.

(50 words)

7. Distance Running

Distance running has become my main sport as I train for an upcoming 10-kilometer race. During my training runs, I focus on steady breathing and rhythmic steps on the pavement. Pushing past mental doubts and physical fatigue to reach the finish line gives me an incredible feeling of personal accomplishment.

(50 words)

8. Volunteer Work

One of my regular weekend activities is volunteering at a YMCA. Every Saturday, I teach children oil painting. I enjoy it because it allows me to meet new people and develop my communication skills. It's a meaningful way to spend my weekends, and I will continue doing it regularly.

(49 words)

9. Visiting a Museum

Last Sunday, I visited the HK Palace Museum with a friend. Have you heard about the Jewellery exhibition? We spent a few hours admiring the beautiful necklaces and brooches. My favourite exhibit was a collection of crowns. I had a great time there. Do you think you'd like to check it out too?

(53 words)

Oral Practice/ Pair Work

Draft the CA script with your CA partner. Use all small talk strategies (see examples below) to complete the first two steps:

  1. Greet your colleague and introduce yourself.
  2. Make small talk on the following topics:
    • Your weekend activities.

1. Echo Question (Show interest)

  • M+ Museum? That sounds interesting!
  • Playing the guitar? That's impressive!
  • You volunteered at a community centre? That sounds meaningful!

2. Wh- Question (Ask for more details)

  • What was your favourite part of the museum visit?
  • What songs are you learning on the guitar?
  • What activities did you help organise during your volunteer work?

💡 Language Tip: Collective Nouns & Subject-Verb Agreement

Pay close attention to collective nouns in business communication:

  • "The purchasing team has already planned..." (singular noun 'team' → has)
  • "The IT department has installed..." (singular noun 'department' → has)
  • "We have designed..." / "They have updated..." (plural pronoun 'we' / 'they' → have)

Project Stage Project Action Item / Scope

What is a collocation?

A collocation is a combination of words that commonly occur together in natural English. In business contexts, using proper collocations makes your communication sound natural and professional.

For example, in English we say 'launch a campaign' (not 'make a campaign') and 'arrange delivery' (not 'give delivery').
